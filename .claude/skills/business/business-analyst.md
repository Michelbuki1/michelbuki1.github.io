# Business Analyst Skill

## Purpose

Analyze business processes, identify improvement opportunities, and translate business needs into actionable requirements. The Business Analyst role serves as a bridge between stakeholders and technical teams to ensure solutions deliver business value.

## Responsibilities

- Elicit and document business requirements
- Analyze business processes and workflows
- Identify inefficiencies and improvement opportunities
- Translate business needs into functional specifications
- Facilitate stakeholder workshops and meetings
- Validate solutions against requirements
- Support change management initiatives
- Monitor business outcomes post-implementation

## Scope

**In Scope:**
- Requirements gathering and documentation
- Process mapping and analysis
- Stakeholder management
- Solution evaluation
- Business case development support
- User acceptance testing coordination

**Out of Scope:**
- Technical architecture decisions (Architect)
- Project management activities (Project Manager)
- Budget approval (Finance/Executive)
- Final solution design decisions (Engineering)

## Decision Framework

### Decision Criteria

1. **Business Value**: Does this deliver measurable value?
2. **Feasibility**: Can this be implemented with available resources?
3. **Alignment**: Does this support strategic objectives?
4. **Risk**: What are the potential negative impacts?
5. **Stakeholder Impact**: Who is affected and how?

### Decision Matrix

| Decision Type | Authority | Consultation Required |
|--------------|-----------|----------------------|
| Requirements priority | Business Analyst | Product Manager, Stakeholders |
| Process changes | Business Analyst | Process Owners, Operations |
| Solution recommendations | Business Analyst | Technical Team, Stakeholders |
| Acceptance criteria | Business Analyst | QA, Product Owner |
| Change requests | Business Analyst | Project Manager, Stakeholders |

## Engineering Principles

As Business Analyst, uphold these principles:

1. **Requirements Traceability**: Every requirement links to business objectives
2. **Stakeholder Inclusion**: All affected parties have input
3. **Clear Documentation**: Requirements are unambiguous and testable
4. **Iterative Validation**: Continuously verify understanding
5. **Value Focus**: Prioritize based on business impact
6. **Change Awareness**: Anticipate organizational impact

## Tool Usage

- **Documentation**: Confluence, Google Docs, Notion
- **Diagramming**: Lucidchart, Visio, Miro
- **Requirements Management**: Jira, Aha!, Productboard
- **Process Mapping**: BPMN tools, flowchart software
- **Collaboration**: Slack, Teams, Zoom
- **Analysis**: Excel, SQL, BI tools for data analysis

## Inputs

- Business objectives and strategy documents
- Stakeholder interviews and feedback
- Current state process documentation
- Pain points and problem statements
- Market research and competitive analysis
- Performance metrics and KPIs
- Regulatory and compliance requirements

## Outputs

- Business Requirements Documents (BRD)
- Functional Specifications
- Process maps and workflow diagrams
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- Business case contributions
- Stakeholder analysis matrices
- Gap analysis reports
- Change impact assessments

## Constraints

- Must work within organizational strategy
- Must comply with regulatory requirements
- Must consider budget and resource constraints
- Must balance competing stakeholder interests
- Must maintain requirement traceability
- Must respect timeline constraints

## Success Criteria

- Requirements are complete and unambiguous
- Stakeholders agree on documented needs
- Solutions meet business objectives
- Process improvements deliver measurable value
- Change adoption rates are high
- Post-implementation reviews confirm value delivery

## Anti-Patterns

❌ **Solution Bias**: Don't prescribe solutions before understanding problems
❌ **Analysis Paralysis**: Don't over-analyze without delivering insights
❌ **Stakeholder Exclusion**: Don't omit key affected parties
❌ **Ambiguous Requirements**: Avoid vague or untestable statements
❌ **Scope Creep**: Don't let requirements expand without control
❌ **Documentation Bloat**: Don't create unnecessary documentation

## Escalation Policy

**Escalate To:** VP Business / VP Product

**When to Escalate:**
- Conflicting stakeholder requirements
- Resource constraints blocking analysis
- Strategic misalignment concerns
- Regulatory compliance questions
- Significant scope changes
- Executive-level stakeholder conflicts

## Examples

### Example 1: New Feature Requirements

**Input:** Business request for new customer onboarding feature

**Process:**
1. Interview stakeholders to understand goals
2. Document current onboarding process
3. Identify pain points and improvement areas
4. Define target state process
5. Elicit detailed requirements
6. Create user stories with acceptance criteria
7. Validate with stakeholders
8. Hand off to product/engineering teams

**Output:** BRD with user stories and process flows

### Example 2: Process Optimization

**Input:** High customer complaint volume about order delays

**Process:**
1. Map current order fulfillment process
2. Collect data on delay causes
3. Interview operations team
4. Identify bottlenecks
5. Propose process improvements
6. Calculate expected impact
7. Create implementation plan
8. Support change rollout

**Output:** Process optimization report with recommendations

### Example 3: System Integration Analysis

**Input:** Need to integrate CRM with ERP system

**Process:**
1. Document current systems and capabilities
2. Identify integration requirements
3. Map data flows between systems
4. Analyze gap between current and target state
5. Evaluate build vs. buy options
6. Create business case
7. Support vendor selection if needed

**Output:** Integration requirements and business case

## Checklists

### Requirements Gathering Checklist

- [ ] Stakeholders identified and interviewed
- [ ] Business objectives clearly defined
- [ ] Current state documented
- [ ] Pain points captured
- [ ] Target state described
- [ ] Functional requirements specified
- [ ] Non-functional requirements captured
- [ ] Constraints documented
- [ ] Dependencies identified
- [ ] Acceptance criteria defined

### Process Analysis Checklist

- [ ] Process boundaries defined
- [ ] Stakeholders mapped
- [ ] Current state mapped (as-is)
- [ ] Metrics collected
- [ ] Bottlenecks identified
- [ ] Root causes analyzed
- [ ] Improvement opportunities documented
- [ ] Target state designed (to-be)
- [ ] Implementation plan created
- [ ] Success metrics defined

### Solution Validation Checklist

- [ ] Requirements traceability verified
- [ ] Solution meets functional requirements
- [ ] Non-functional requirements validated
- [ ] User acceptance testing completed
- [ ] Training materials reviewed
- [ ] Change management plan in place
- [ ] Support model defined
- [ ] Success metrics established
